Behavioral Measurement Survey: Pilot Sequence

Try the pilot yourself

In the pilot, 100 German residents recruited via Prolific completed a survey and six decision tasks, two each for risk tolerance, prosociality and patience.

In each task, participants earned points depending on their decisions. A task was selected at random for payment, at a rate of £0.50 per point.

Each link below opens one step of the sequence on its own, in the platform’s demo mode, with the parameters the pilot used. The demos open in German, as in the pilot; you can switch to English in the demo’s sidebar.

The sequence

In the order participants saw it. Each step opens in a new tab.

  1. Questionnaire

    Education and work, finances, everyday behaviour and demographics: the outcomes the preference measures are related to. Two of the questions are attention checks.

    Open demo

  2. Dictator Game, 15 points

    Split 15 points between yourself and another participant.

    Open demo

  3. Multiple Price Lists: Time

    In each row, choose between an amount today and a larger amount later.

    Open demo

  4. Multiple Price Lists: Risk

    In each row, choose between a sure amount and a 50:50 lottery.

    Open demo

  5. Convex Time Budgets

    With a slider, split a budget between an earlier and a later date, at varying exchange rates.

    Open demo

  6. Bomb Risk Elicitation Task

    Collect boxes from a grid of 100. Each box adds to your reward, but one hides a bomb that wipes out your earnings in this task.

    Open demo

  7. Dictator Game, 20 points

    The decision from task 1 again, now with 20 points to split between yourself and another participant.

    Open demo
